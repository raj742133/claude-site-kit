# Brag Plan: Sitewright

## What is this app?
Sitewright is a Claude Code skill that turns "tell Claude your brand" into a complete, working Next.js website (landing page, sign-in, MFA, dashboard, publishing, connect-a-phone) with a real backend, then proves it works by driving it in a real browser.

## The angle
A quiet, confident product film for a developer tool people should trust. The product's own promise is the whole script: *Tell Claude your brand. Get a working website.* The video is the proof in three beats, all taken from the real product and its real output: a request typed to Claude, modules ticked off, and a finished site that has already passed 87 of 87 browser checks. No jokes, no hype words; the restraint is the point, and the evidence does the selling.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The site's own headline, set huge on a pale canvas. "Tell Claude your brand." types in with a caret. At the first strong beat (3.7s) the second line, "Get a working website.", rises in, in the brand blue. Two sentences, no logo yet.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The request: a dark terminal card; "Use Sitewright to make a landing page, a dashboard and a connect page for Bean & Barrel." types out, Claude answers "Which modules do you want?", and four modules tick on one by one: Landing page, Dashboard, Connect to phone, Sign in.
- The proof: "Generated, built, and driven in a browser." The count climbs to 87 / 87 checks passed while real screenshots of the generated Bean & Barrel site arrive one at a time: landing page, orders dashboard, phone.
- The range: "One kit. Any brand." Four real generated sites side by side (Bean & Barrel, Halden Legal, Sunny Side Bakery, Pixel Studio), each with its own colours and logo.

## Outro / punchline
The hexagon mark draws itself, then "Sitewright", then "A Claude Code skill that builds your website." and the address. Music fades under it into near-silence.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: type the request to Claude ("Use Sitewright to make a landing page, a dashboard and a connect page for ...").
2. Key action: Claude asks which modules; four are ticked; the site is generated and driven in Chromium.
3. Result: a working site (landing, dashboard, phone layout) and "87 / 87 checks passed".

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: four scenes with long holds, slow 0.7s crossfades, mixed-case light-to-medium type at very large scale, one idea per scene, and no lists beyond the four module ticks.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 22.5 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f3f5fa (page), #ffffff (cards), #0d1424 (terminal)
- Accent: #1e5bd8 (primary blue), #14a6cc (signal cyan)
- Text: #0d1424 (ink), #55607a (muted)
- Display font: Montserrat (700-800 for headlines, 500 for lines)
- Body font: JetBrains Mono (terminal and labels)
- Strongest visual element: the site's own hero headline in Montserrat on the pale canvas, the hexagon-and-lens mark, and the real screenshots of generated sites in browser and phone frames.

## Share copy (draft)
Introducing Sitewright: tell Claude your brand, get a working website. Landing page, sign-in, MFA, dashboard and publishing, tested in a real browser.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (clean, mid-tempo, CC BY 4.0; credit in the README)
- Music treatment: starts at 0:00, bed around 0.55 volume, fade-in over 0.8s, fade-out from 20.8s to 22.4s so the last half-second is near silence
- Music cue guidance: preset read (115 BPM, beat every 0.52s, grid 1.60, 2.12, 2.65, ...). Strong cues to lock: 3.70s (second hook line), 9.50s (module ticks complete), 12.65s (check count lands), 17.91s (brand row reveal). Sequential items snap to every other beat so text stays readable.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to let the background glow and the hexagon mark breathe. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, soft
- Audio-coupled moments: key ticks while the two typed lines appear; a soft interface tick for each module check; a quiet card sound for each screenshot arriving; one low bell-like hit when the mark completes.
- Restraint rule: nothing loud, no whooshes on every transition, and audio must not mask the final fade to near silence.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - Hook - 5.0s (0.0-5.0)
Pale #f3f5fa canvas with a faint dotted field and a soft blue radial glow. Line 1 "Tell Claude your brand." types in (large, ink, weight 700) with a caret. At 3.7s line 2 "Get a working website." rises in below it in #1e5bd8. A small hexagon mark and "Sitewright" sit top-left. Both lines settled for at least 1.3s before the crossfade.
Sequential/interaction: yes - line 1 types character by character; line 2 follows on the strong beat.
Audio intent: attention without drama.
Audio-coupled idea: soft key ticks on the typed line; one low accent as line 2 lands.
Music: bed fades in.
Transition mood: soft crossfade (0.7s) -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 - The request - 6.2s (4.3-10.5)
A dark #0d1424 terminal card (JetBrains Mono, very large) slides in from the left, bar reading "claude - Sitewright". "you" line types: "Use Sitewright to make a landing page, a dashboard and a connect page for Bean & Barrel." Then "claude  Which modules do you want?" and four checks tick on in turn: [x] Landing page, [x] Dashboard, [x] Connect to phone, [x] Sign in. To the right, large light-weight text: "Pick the pieces. Skip the rest." The full set of ticks holds for at least 1.5s.
Sequential/interaction: yes - typed request, then four module ticks one by one (snap to every other beat, hold the full set afterwards).
Audio intent: the feeling of a clean tool doing exactly what was asked.
Audio-coupled idea: key ticks while typing; one soft interface tick per module check.
Transition mood: soft crossfade -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 - The proof - 6.0s (10.0-16.0)
Heading top-left: "Generated, built, and driven in a browser." A big stat counts "87 / 87" with the label "checks passed" (real result for the Bean & Barrel site). Real screenshots of the generated site arrive one at a time in frames: the landing page (browser frame), the orders dashboard (overlapping browser frame) and the phone layout (phone frame). All screenshots come from the repository's own showcase captures; the brand and data in them are fictional.
Sequential/interaction: yes - three screenshots arrive one after another (every other beat), the counter climbs during the first.
Audio intent: quiet satisfaction.
Audio-coupled idea: soft card sound per screenshot; a single clean tick as the count lands.
Transition mood: soft crossfade -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 - The range and outro - 6.5s (15.5-22.0, hold to 22.5)
Beat A (15.5-19.2): "One kit. Any brand." with four real landing pages in a row, each a different brand: Bean & Barrel (brown and amber), Halden Legal (navy and gold), Sunny Side Bakery (yellow), Pixel Studio (purple and cyan), arriving one by one.
Beat B (19.2-22.5): the cards clear; the hexagon mark draws itself; "Sitewright" large in Montserrat 800; "A Claude Code skill that builds your website." beneath; the address `sitewright-skill.vercel.app` in JetBrains Mono. Hold. Music fades to near silence.
Sequential/interaction: yes - four brand cards one by one; then the mark draws.
Audio intent: confidence, then calm.
Audio-coupled idea: card sounds for the brand cards (softest option); one low bell as the mark completes.
Transition mood: soft crossfade (A to B)

**Music mood for this video:** warm, clean, mid-tempo
**Audio summary:** A soft bed under typed ticks and quiet interface sounds, one low bell at the logo, then a long fade to near silence.
